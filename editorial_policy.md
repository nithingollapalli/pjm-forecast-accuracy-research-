# IEEE Spectrum editorial policies checked — November 2026

Sources: [Expert authors](https://spectrum.ieee.org/guidelines-expert-authors), [Freelancers](https://spectrum.ieee.org/guidelines-freelancers), [Generative AI statement](https://spectrum.ieee.org/about).

- Technical experts: send a **few-paragraph proposal**, not a completed manuscript. Distinct firsthand technical insight is preferred; explain methods, limits, and why you are qualified. Experts are **not paid**; Spectrum features get DOIs and are on IEEE Xplore.
- Expert-written features normally ~1,500–3,000 words; shorter guest pieces ~800–1,500 words.
- For Power, Energy & Climate, the listed beat editor is **Emily Waltz** (e.waltz@ieee.org); submit to **one editor**, not several concurrently.
- Humans must report, write, edit and fact-check all articles. AI may help with *ideation and background research*, but facts must be human verified. Avoid using AI-generated prose unchanged. Disclose material AI involvement to the editor and ask about code/figure requirements before submission.
- IEEE journals/conference papers have their own AI-disclosure policies; do not infer that every rule for scholarly manuscripts automatically replaces Spectrum's editorial process.
- Editorial acceptance, DOI, and O-1A evidentiary significance are not guaranteed.
